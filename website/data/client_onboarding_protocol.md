# 📋 VantorX Client Onboarding SOP & Action Protocol

> **TRIGGER:** Whenever the founder says *"I signed [Client Name / Company]"*, immediately output their customized 48-Hour Execution To-Do List across these 5 phases.

---

### Phase 1: Minute 1 — Instant Welcome & 4-Question Intake
- [ ] Send welcome message with payment confirmation.
- [ ] Collect the **4 Core Intake Details**:
  1. **Owner / Dispatch Phone:** Where priority emergency SMS leads should buzz.
  2. **Service Territory:** Exact zip codes, cities, or mileage radius.
  3. **Pricing Rules:** Standard diagnostic fee & emergency baseline rates.
  4. **Calendar Link:** Google Calendar, Outlook, or Cal.com scheduling link.

---

### Phase 2: Hour 12 — Vapi Voice AI Customization
- [ ] Duplicate master VantorX Ava Voice template in Vapi.
- [ ] Set business name: `"[Client Company Name]"`.
- [ ] Insert diagnostic fee, service radius, and trade specialties into prompt.
- [ ] Enable bilingual auto-detection (English & Spanish).
- [ ] Assign dedicated inbound tracking phone number.

---

### Phase 3: Hour 24 — LeadFlow SMS & Webhook Wiring
- [ ] Connect Vapi `"end-of-call-report"` webhook to Make/Zapier.
- [ ] Configure Owner Emergency Alert SMS template:
  `🚨 NEW [TRADE] LEAD: [Name] at [Address]. Issue: [Summary]. Phone: [Phone]`
- [ ] Configure Customer Auto-Confirmation SMS template:
  `Hey [Name], [Company] here! Ava confirmed your request. Our on-call tech has your file.`
- [ ] Configure 2-hour post-job Google Review Accelerator SMS link.

---

### Phase 4: Hour 36 — Mobile Website & Ballpark Estimator
- [ ] Configure client trade subdomain / custom domain.
- [ ] Update all 1-tap call buttons to the new Ava tracking number.
- [ ] Calibrate the Interactive Ballpark Price Estimator to client's average job tickets.
- [ ] Run Core Web Vitals audit to confirm sub-second (<0.8s) load time.

---

### Phase 5: Hour 48 — 5-Minute Handshake Call & Call Forwarding
- [ ] Schedule 5-minute speakerphone test call with owner.
- [ ] Have owner dial the live Ava line from another phone.
- [ ] Verify test lead SMS lands on owner's cell within 10 seconds.
- [ ] Guide owner through carrier Conditional Call Forwarding (*71 on Verizon/AT&T/T-Mobile).
- [ ] Mark client active in Client Hub!
