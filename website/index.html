FAQ

Common questions.

Do I have to set anything up myself?

No. VantorX is done-for-you. Our team builds your website, configures your phone system and lead follow-up, tests everything, and goes live with you. You can share information with us however is easiest.

Can I just get a website without the AI receptionist?

Yes. Website design and development is a standalone service. You can add the AI receptionist, LeadFlow, Client Hub and automation any time — or from day one.

Will my website be mobile-friendly?

Yes. Every site we build is designed to work well on phones, tablets and desktops.

Who updates my website after it launches?

We do. We manage and update your site for you — hours, services, pricing, photos and more — so you don’t have to touch it.

What happens to the leads from my website?

Where applicable, your site’s forms connect to LeadFlow so every inquiry is captured and followed up, and visible in your Client Hub.

How long does setup take?

It depends on what you’re getting. We’ll give you a clear timeline when we scope your project on the demo call.
