FAQ

Common questions.

Everything you need to know about our packages, implementation, and guarantees.

How does the 3-tier pricing work vs. buying a la carte?

Our Essential ($397/mo + $497 setup), Accelerate ($697/mo + $997 setup), and Scale ($1,297/mo + $1,997 setup) bundles give you complete business systems at steep bundled savings (saving up to $494/mo). However, if you only need one specific solution, every single service is available standalone a la carte—such as our 24/7 AI Receptionist ($397/mo + $497 setup), Custom Website Design ($799 flat upfront), AEO Engine ($297/mo + $297 setup), or LeadFlow CRM ($197/mo + $197 setup).

Why is the Accelerate Tier ($697/mo) the most popular choice?

Accelerate solves both sides of local business growth: capturing traffic and converting callers. You get a brand-new custom website, AEO Engine to rank on ChatGPT and Perplexity, LeadFlow CRM & missed-call text-back, and our 24/7 AI Receptionist to answer and book every caller. Buying these separately costs over $1,190/mo, so Accelerate saves you nearly $500 every single month.

What does the One-Time Implementation & Setup Fee cover?

The one-time onboarding fee covers complete white-glove engineering: custom voice AI prompt training with your specific trade terminology and service pricing, carrier forwarding (*71) and Twilio routing, calendar and SMS integration, test calls, and (for Accelerate & Scale) custom website design, architecture, and AEO schema. Everything is 100% white-glove and done-for-you with zero recurring lock-in contracts.

Can I start with Essential and upgrade later?

Yes, absolutely. Many clients start on Essential ($397/mo) to stop losing after-hours calls, then upgrade to Accelerate ($697/mo) or Scale ($1,297/mo) as their lead volume and booking capacity expand. There are zero long-term contracts and you can adjust your plan anytime directly inside your Client Hub.

Do I have to configure any software myself?

No. VantorX is 100% done-for-you. Our team builds your website, configures your phone system and lead follow-up, tests everything with you, and goes live. You don't have to learn, patch, or manage complex software.

Will my website be mobile-friendly and fast?

Yes. Every site we build is engineered specifically for phones, tablets, and desktops with sub-second loading (<0.8s) and 100/100 Core Web Vitals mobile scores for maximum tap-to-call conversions.

Who updates my website and emergency menu after launch?

We do. We manage and update your site for you — emergency hours, service pricing, photos, and trade seasonal specials — so you can focus 100% on job sites and running your crews.

What happens to the leads from my website and calls?

Every form submission, calculator estimate, and phone call flows directly into your private Client Hub with instant SMS alerts dispatched to your personal cell, so no emergency lead ever slips through the cracks.
