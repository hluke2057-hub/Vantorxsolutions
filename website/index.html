FAQ

Common questions.

How does the 3-tier pricing work vs. buying a la carte?

Our Essential ($397/mo + $497 setup), Accelerate ($697/mo + $997 setup), and Scale ($1,297/mo + $1,997 setup) bundles give you complete business systems at steep bundled savings (saving up to $494/mo). However, if you only need one specific solution, every single service is available standalone a la carte—such as our 24/7 AI Receptionist ($397/mo + $497 setup), Custom Website Design ($799 flat), AEO Engine ($297/mo + $297 setup), or LeadFlow CRM & Pipeline ($197/mo + $197 setup).

Why is the Accelerate Tier ($697/mo) the most popular choice?

Accelerate solves both sides of local business growth: capturing traffic and converting callers. You get a brand-new custom website, AEO Engine to rank on ChatGPT and Perplexity, LeadFlow CRM & missed-call text-back, and our 24/7 AI Receptionist to answer and book every caller. Buying these separately costs over $1,190/mo, so Accelerate saves you nearly $500 every single month.

What does the One-Time Implementation & Setup Fee cover?

The one-time onboarding fee covers complete white-glove engineering: custom voice AI prompt training with your specific services and trade terminology, phone carrier forwarding and Twilio routing, calendar and SMS integration, test calls, and (for Accelerate & Scale) custom website design, architecture, and AEO schema. Everything is 100% white-glove and done-for-you with zero recurring lock-in contracts.

Can I start with Essential and upgrade later?

Yes, absolutely. Many clients start on Essential ($397/mo) to stop losing after-hours calls, then upgrade to Accelerate ($697/mo) or Scale ($1,297/mo) as their lead volume and booking capacity expand. There are zero long-term contracts and you can adjust your plan anytime.

Do I have to set anything up myself?

No. VantorX is 100% done-for-you. Our team builds your website, configures your phone system and lead follow-up, tests everything, and goes live with you. You don't have to learn or manage software.

Will my website be mobile-friendly and fast?

Yes. Every site we build is engineered specifically for phones, tablets and desktops with instant loading and tap-to-call conversion.

Who updates my website and menu after it launches?

We do. We manage and update your site for you — hours, services, pricing, photos and more — so you don’t have to touch it.

What happens to the leads from my website and calls?

Every form submission and phone call flows directly into your private Client Hub with instant SMS alerts to your personal cell, so no customer ever slips through the cracks.
