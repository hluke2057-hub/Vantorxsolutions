FAQ

Common questions.

How does the 3-tier pricing work vs. buying a la carte?

Our Essential ($299/mo), Accelerate ($499/mo), and Scale ($799/mo) bundles give you complete business systems at steep bundled savings (saving up to $198/mo). However, if you only need one specific solution, every single service is available standalone a la carte—such as our 24/7 AI Receptionist ($299/mo), Custom Website Design ($199/mo), AEO Engine ($199/mo), or LeadFlow SMS ($149/mo).

Why is the Accelerate Tier ($499/mo) the most popular choice?

Accelerate solves both sides of local business growth: capturing traffic and converting callers. You get a brand-new custom website, AEO Engine to rank on ChatGPT and Perplexity, LeadFlow missed-call text-back, and our 24/7 AI Receptionist to answer and book every caller. Buying these separately costs $697/mo, so Accelerate saves you nearly $200 every single month.

Can I start with Essential and upgrade later?

Yes, absolutely. Many clients start on Essential ($299/mo) to stop losing after-hours calls, then upgrade to Accelerate ($499/mo) or Scale ($799/mo) as their lead volume and booking capacity expand. There are zero contracts and you can adjust your plan anytime.

Do I have to set anything up myself?

No. VantorX is 100% done-for-you. Our team builds your website, configures your phone system and lead follow-up, tests everything, and goes live with you. You don't have to learn or manage software.

Will my website be mobile-friendly and fast?

Yes. Every site we build is engineered specifically for phones, tablets and desktops with instant loading and tap-to-call conversion.

Who updates my website and menu after it launches?

We do. We manage and update your site for you — hours, services, pricing, photos and more — so you don’t have to touch it.

What happens to the leads from my website and calls?

Every form submission and phone call flows directly into your private Client Hub with instant SMS alerts to your personal cell, so no customer ever slips through the cracks.
