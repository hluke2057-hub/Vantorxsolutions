LEGAL COMPLIANCE & PRIVACY DOCUMENTATION

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 26, 2026 · Operating Entity: VantorX Solutions LLC · Contact: privacy@vantorxsolutions.com

VantorX Solutions ("VantorX", "we", "us", or "our") provides business infrastructure, website development, customer relationship management (LeadFlow), and automated phone receptionist services (Ava) to commercial clients and trade contractors nationwide. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, process, and protect your information across our website (www.vantorxsolutions.com), client platforms, and telephony services.

Notice at Collection: We collect personal Contact Information, Commercial & Business Details, and Voice Call Audio & Transcripts when you interact with our website, book a demo, or call our AI phone receptionist (Ava). Under CalOPPA, CCPA / CPRA, and applicable state laws, we do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for cross-context behavioral advertising.

1. Categories of Personal Information We Collect

We collect personal information directly from you, automatically through your interaction with our digital platforms, and via inbound telephony communications:

  • Contact Information: Full name, company name, email address, telephone number, and physical business location provided through demo forms or intake consultations.
  • Business Details: Operating hours, service menus, pricing schedules, staff dispatch contacts, and CRM pipeline metadata.
  • Voice Call Audio & Transcripts: Inbound caller phone numbers (Caller ID), call timestamps, call durations, live voice audio streams, automated speech-to-text transcripts, appointment bookings, and emergency triage notes processed by our AI receptionist.
  • Technical & Internet Activity: IP addresses, browser types, device telemetry, referring URLs, and local configuration preferences stored on your device via localStorage.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your personal information strictly for legitimate commercial and operational purposes:

  • To deliver, configure, and operate your 24/7 AI Phone Receptionist (Ava) and automate calendar appointment bookings.
  • To route emergency service calls and dispatch instant SMS alerts to on-call technicians under express consent.
  • To process subscription billing, recurring invoices, and account administration via Stripe.
  • To provide customer support, system updates, and quality assurance audits.
  • To comply with state and federal legal obligations, including tax, wiretapping (Fla. Stat. § 934.03), and telecommunications regulations.

3. Artificial Intelligence (AI) Voice Processing & Model Disclosures

VantorX utilizes modern Artificial Intelligence (AI) voice processing, Large Language Models, and automated voice synthesis systems:

  • Automated Conversational Voice AI: When you call our phone systems or client business lines, calls are answered and processed by our conversational voice agent (Ava). Callers receive an immediate opening disclosure that the call may be recorded for quality and training purposes.
  • Large Language Models & Voice Synthesis: Audio streams are converted to text via speech recognition, processed through Large Language Models to interpret intent, and responded to via low-latency voice synthesis.
  • No Public Foundation Model Training: We do not permit sub-processors to use customer voice data or client conversations to train public foundation models. Your business data remains isolated and proprietary.
  • Safety & Crisis Guardrails: Our AI models include strict safety filters. The AI cannot dispatch emergency 911 services. If mental health distress or self-harm keywords are detected, the system immediately provides the National 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.

5. Data Retention & User Upload Deletion Schedule

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill operational obligations and provide services:

  • 90-Day Voice Audio Retention: Inbound call voice audio recordings are retained for quality assurance and training for a maximum of 90 days, after which all audio files are automatically and permanently purged from active storage.
  • Transcripts & CRM Contacts: Text transcripts and customer contact entries are retained during active client subscriptions to preserve historical customer records.
  • Right to Delete & User Upload Purging: You have the absolute right to request deletion of your account data and any uploaded documents. Submit erasure requests to privacy@vantorxsolutions.com (or privacy@vantorx.com). Verified requests are fulfilled within 30 calendar days.

6. Consumer Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA, CalOPPA & GDPR)

In compliance with the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), CalOPPA, and international standards, consumers possess the following enforceable rights:

  • Right to Know & Access: You may request disclosure of the specific categories and pieces of personal information collected.
  • Right to Delete: You have the right to request deletion of your personal data without penalty.
  • Right to Correct: You may request rectification of inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale: We do not sell personal information or share personal data for behavioral profiling.
  • Non-Discrimination: We will never penalize or charge higher rates to any customer who exercises their privacy rights.

7. Storage Bucket Security & Encryption Safeguards

We enforce rigorous administrative, technical, and architectural security standards:

  • Encryption Standards: All data is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS) and encrypted at rest using AES-256 standard encryption.
  • Row-Level Security (RLS) Tenant Isolation: Every database table enforces Supabase Row-Level Security (RLS) policies, ensuring multi-tenant isolation where clients can only query their own authenticated records.
  • Private by Default Storage Buckets: We maintain strictly private cloud storage buckets with zero public unrestricted access. Storage objects require authenticated, signed session tokens to view or download.

8. Children's Online Privacy (COPPA)

Our website and business infrastructure services are strictly intended for commercial entities and individuals aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under 13.
