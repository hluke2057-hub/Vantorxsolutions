"I'll be super quick: I was looking at your Google profile in [City]. In your trade, when an emergency job calls at 8 PM on a Saturday, most guys send it to voicemail and lose the $8,000 job to the next competitor on Google.

👉 If he says "Answering service": "Those call centers usually take 15 minutes to relay a message, and half the time they misspell the address. That's exactly why we built Ava."

🥊 Stage 2: Instant Objection Rebuttal Bank (Click to Open While on Call)

❌ 1. "Just send me an email." Brush-Off
"I'm happy to do that, [Name]. But let's be honest — my email is just going to sit at the bottom of your inbox behind 50 supply orders and receipts.

Literally take 15 seconds to dial our live test line: (561) 813-8602. Hear Ava answer in 1 ring. If you don't love it, you never have to reply to me again."
💡 Radical honesty: Acknowledges email is useless and challenges them to immediate proof.
❌ 2. "We already have a receptionist / answering service." Existing Staff
"That's awesome — she's probably incredible from 8 AM to 5 PM! But what happens between 6 PM and 7 AM, or all day Saturday and Sunday?

Over 35% of emergency calls happen when your office is closed. Ava doesn't replace your receptionist; she covers the off-hours so you stop donating after-hours revenue to your competitors."
💡 Never criticize staff. Frame Ava as after-hours coverage that relieves them.
❌ 3. "How much is this going to cost me?" Price Screen
"Zero contracts, starting at $299 a month. In your trade, one single emergency job pays for the entire year.

But don't buy anything right now — just dial (561) 813-8602 from your cell and see if she's even worth 10 cents to your business."
💡 Extreme ROI contrast. One $8,000 job dwarfs $299.
❌ 4. "AI sounds robotic and fake — my customers would hate that." Tech Cynic
"I 100% agree with you — the robotic phone trees from 2 years ago drive customers away. Ava is conversational voice AI with zero latency. She breathes and pauses naturally.

Punch in (561) 813-8602 right now from your cell. If you can tell she's AI within the first 30 seconds, I'll never call you again."
💡 Agree with them first, then challenge them to prove it wrong.
❌ 5. "I'm slammed on a job / driving right now." Time Pressure
"I completely hear you — that's exactly why we do 100% of the build for you with zero software to learn.

I'll let you get back to work: when you park, just dial (561) 813-8602 for 30 seconds. Can I shoot you a quick 1-line text with the number so you have it in your phone?"
💡 Removes pressure and gets permission to text them your demo number.
❌ 6. "We have plenty of work / not looking for more leads." Capacity
"That's the best position to be in, [Name]! But this isn't about getting more leads — it's about reputation and high-ticket emergencies.

When your best repeat customer calls you on a Saturday with an emergency and gets your voicemail, they get frustrated. Ava ensures your existing VIP clients get answered in 1 ring 24/7."
💡 Reframes from 'sales leads' to customer retention and VIP reputation.
❌ 7. "How did you get my number / is this a scam?" Suspicion
"I found your listing right on Google in [City] — your phone number is public right on your business profile!

I'm based right here in South Florida. I'm not asking for your credit card or selling you anything right now. I literally just wanted you to hear Ava on (561) 813-8602 and see what's possible."
💡 Radical transparency. Proves you are a real local person.
❌ 8. "I need to talk to my business partner / wife first." Decision Stall
"100% — you should definitely talk to them! But don't try to explain voice AI to them yourself.

Put them on speakerphone tonight and dial (561) 813-8602 together. Test her for 60 seconds. If both of you love it, let's talk tomorrow."
💡 Eliminates second-hand pitch telephone. The test line does the selling.
❌ 9. "We don't do after-hours emergency calls." Service Mismatch
"Got it! Then Ava is even more valuable for you during the day when you're busy.

When your guys are on a roof or under a house and the phone rings, Ava answers in 1 ring, takes their info, and sends you a clean text summary so you don't lose the daytime estimate."
💡 Pivots to daytime missed-call text-back.
❌ 10. "I'm not interested." (Instant Cold Hang-Up Defense) Hard No
"Fair enough, [Name] — I'll get out of your hair!

Before I hang up, just save this in your cell: (561) 813-8602. If you ever have a weekend where you're tired of answering emergency calls while eating dinner, dial it."
💡 Exit with grace. Leaves your number planted in their mind.
