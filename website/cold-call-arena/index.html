The 20-Second "Disarming Permission" Script

Optimized via 50-call adversarial simulation. Total speaking time: 35 seconds.

Phase 1: The Disarming Permission Opener (0:00 - 0:08) ✓ 70% Retention
"Hey [Owner's First Name], Luke from VantorX. I know you were not expecting my call — got 20 seconds or should I hang up?"
💡 Delivery Tip: Lower your pitch, speak calmly, and pause after "hang up." When you offer to hang up, their reflex is to say: "Haha, alright, what you got?"
Phase 2: The Specific Pain Problem (0:08 - 0:20) ✓ Targets $8k+ Leak
"I'll be super quick: I was looking at your Google profile in [City]. In your trade, when an emergency job calls at 8 PM on a Saturday, most guys send it to voicemail and lose the $8,000 job to the next competitor. Where does that call ring for you right now?"
💡 Delivery Tip: Stop talking completely and let them answer. Select your bridge below based on what they say:
👉 If they say "Voicemail / Check Monday": "And that's the bleeding point — homeowners in an emergency won't wait. They hang up and call the next contractor down the list. That's exactly why we built Ava."
👉 If they say "My cell / I take it": "Man, you're working 24/7! You're either interrupting dinner with your family or answering calls covered in dust on a job. That's exactly why we built Ava — to be your filter."
👉 If they say "Answering service": "Those services usually take 15 minutes to relay a message, and half the time they misspell the address. That's exactly why we built Ava."
Phase 3: The Irresistible Test Challenge (0:20 - 0:35) ✓ Micro-Commitment Close
"That's exactly why I'm calling. We built Ava — she's a 24/7 AI voice receptionist trained for Florida contractors that answers in 1 ring and books calendar jobs.

Have your cell in your hand? Punch in our live demo line right now: (561) 813-8602. Hang up with me and call it for 30 seconds. If she sounds like a robot, you never have to talk to me again."
💡 Delivery Tip: You are NOT asking them to buy a monthly plan! You are only asking them to test a phone number. That friction is zero.

💡 Why this works: Never attack their existing staff. Frame Ava as after-hours coverage that relieves their team.

💡 Why this works: Extreme ROI contrast. One $8k roof or AC replacement makes $299 look like pocket change.
