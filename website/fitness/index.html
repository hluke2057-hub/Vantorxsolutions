THE FOUNDATIONAL PRINCIPLE

"Adding weight is progressive overload. If you're not tracking reps and adding weight, you're just exercising—not building muscle."

Most guys do 4 sets of 12 with weight they could easily do for 18. In this program, every set is taken to absolute mechanical failure (RPE 10). When you train with true intensity, 2 working sets triggers 100% of muscle protein synthesis with zero wasted recovery capacity.
